Burly is a lifting tracker built for Apple Watch. Start a routine on your wrist, log each set with a tap, and let the rest timer tell you when to go again. Your iPhone is where you plan routines and look back at your training.

ON APPLE WATCH
• Log sets from your wrist, with your last weight and reps right there
• A rest timer between sets
• Finish or discard a workout, or pick it up again where you left off
• Save each finished workout to Apple Health, with heart rate and energy from the watch's sensors, if you allow it

ON IPHONE
• Build routines from a catalog of exercises, or add your own
• History grouped by week, with an editor for sets, warm-ups, exercises and notes
• Stats for exercise progression, weekly volume, muscle split and consistency
• Kilograms or pounds, and your own default rest time
• Import your history from a Hevy CSV export, with a preview before anything is saved

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your workouts stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch, which talk to each other directly. Burly itself never sends your data anywhere.
