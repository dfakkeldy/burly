# Burly privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026.

Burly is a lifting tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is built so that
your data stays with you.

## What Burly collects

Nothing. Burly has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and
no tracking. The developer never receives your workouts or anything else
about you, and Burly shares nothing with third parties.

## What Burly stores on your devices

- **Your training:** routines, exercises, logged workouts and sets, notes,
  and your settings (such as kilograms or pounds and the default rest time).
  These live in Burly's own storage on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
- **Between your iPhone and Apple Watch:** routines and finished workouts
  travel directly between your own paired devices through Apple's
  WatchConnectivity framework. They do not pass through any server of ours.
- **Hevy import:** if you import a Hevy CSV export, Burly reads the file you
  choose and saves the workouts in its own storage. Imported workouts are
  never written to Apple Health.

Your device's own backups (iCloud or a computer) may include Burly's
storage, as they do for other apps. Apple handles those backups under its
own privacy policy.

## Apple Health

Burly asks for Health access on Apple Watch the first time you start a
workout. If you allow it:

- Burly **writes** each finished workout to Health, so other apps you allow
  can see it.
- Burly **reads** heart rate and active energy from your watch's sensors
  during a workout, to record them with that workout.

Health data is used only for these purposes. It is never used for
advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone. If you
decline, Burly still logs and saves your workouts; nothing else changes. You
can change Burly's access at any time in the Health app under Sharing, then
Apps, then Burly.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Burly from a device deletes its storage on that device. Workouts
Burly saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.
