Proposed direction: The Burly Strongman

A vintage strongman presses a loaded bar overhead in Prizefighter Red.

Master mark
Design contract

Idea

Strength with a protagonist

A larger-than-life strongman makes Burly feel like a character. The overhead press identifies the lifting category without relying on another standalone barbell.

Construction

One flat silhouette, a wide triangular stance, rounded vintage plates, and two tiny parchment cuts for the singlet and moustache. No text, gradients, borders, or baked-in corner mask.
